# HangOut-
This is project 6 for Mobile Software Development! Our app is a fun variation of Hang Man! Our task was to create an interesting app using the Firebase Cloud Storage to allow for multiple users to access information and interact with each other in some way. What you see here is the culmination of the hard work that Drew Williams, Jordan Buchanan, and Amiko Kozmanishvili have achieved as a group. We hope you enjoy, Hang-Out!

- The main audience for this app is anyone who enjoys Hangman in a casual or competitive stance. There is a leaderboard that displays the highest amount of fails per individual word. Each person can only input 1 word a day, thus, the goal of the app is to add the most difficult word you can think of and have people fail your word to get you onto the leaderboard. So bring your creativity and pop open a dictionary. The more difficult the word the higher more fails you will get. If you don't want to create your own word that's also okay! You can play casually because the word you do gets randomized to you and you can play as much as you want to!

- The app has wide spread of usage. First, you must login or Signup to our firebase cloud storage. Then you go to the main menu screen which has a Leaderboard of the top 5 words with the most failed attempts, a start game button, and an add word popup widget. Each account can input one word a day. The word just has to be between 3-15 characters. The word must also be spelled correctly or it wont work. After this, you can move on to the actual game page. An opponent is chosen at random and you will try and guess their word. After 5 guesses, the display will update as you fail then take you to the game over page. If you win, it will congratulate you on completion, if you lose, it will condemn you. Either case, the word and opponent gets displayed on the final screen. Additionally, you will be given the option to sign out, or go back to the main menu and try another word.

- This app is useful because it is a very fun and appealing game. We believe the word restricting time function allows for a new way of playing the game. This creates the idea that volume doesn't matter as much as complexity of a single word. Our app is unique in this aspect which increases its usefulness. Overall, it could be built upon and edited into a fairly successful app!
